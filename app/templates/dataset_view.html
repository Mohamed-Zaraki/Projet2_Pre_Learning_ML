Noise & Outlier Takeaways
  • Without Outliers (IQR): Trimming extreme statistical anomalies stabilizes decision boundaries and improves generalization.
  • Feature Noise Vulnerability: Distance-based algorithms (like KNN) suffer when continuous features undergo Gaussian perturbation.
  • Gaussian Smoothing (σ=1.2): Applies gaussian_filter1d column-by-column to suppress noise before training — often recovers lost accuracy.
  • Ensemble Robustness: Random Forest averages multiple randomized trees, significantly mitigating noise and isolated outlier spikes.
